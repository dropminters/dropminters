Who can mint?

Only wallets holding SPACE ID Agent ID on BNB Chain. EOAs only.

How does AI agent mint work?

Copy the mint prompt, paste to your agent. It follows skill.md, mines PoW, shows a preview, waits for your confirm.

Airdrop?

All DropMinters holders (free and paid) get a future airdrop from the creator.

What do I pay?

Free mint: 0.0005 BNB mining fee + gas. Paid mint (when enabled): 0.005 BNB each (live from contract when loaded), max 2 per wallet.
